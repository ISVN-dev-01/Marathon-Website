Race Essentials FAQ

BIBs are available for collection at the Yendada Marathon Expo held the day before the event at Hotel Novotel, Visakhapatnam. Expo hours are 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM. Bring a printed or digital copy of your registration confirmation email, along with a valid photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport).

For runners who miss the expo, a limited BIB collection window is available at the race venue on race day from 04:00 AM – 05:30 AM. After 05:30 AM, uncollected BIBs may be forfeited.

A complimentary baggage drop facility is available at the start area from 04:00 AM. Use only official Yendada Marathon bag-drop bags (provided in your race kit). Label your bag clearly with your BIB number.

We strongly advise against carrying valuables on race day. The organisers accept no responsibility for lost or damaged items. Bags are available for collection from 08:00 AM after the finish. Please present your BIB tag to collect.

  • 2KM Fun Run — Open to all ages. A great entry point for anyone new to running.
  • 5KM Fit India Run — Minimum age: 14 years.
  • 10KM Endurance Run — Minimum age: 18 years. Runners aged 18–21 require a signed parental consent form.

All registrations are non-refundable. However, entry transfers to another person are permitted up to 7 days before race day. Submit a transfer request via the official website. A ₹100 transfer fee applies.

Category upgrades or downgrades are allowed (subject to availability) until 10 days before the event. Any difference in registration fee is either charged or credited as applicable.

In the event of race cancellation due to unforeseen circumstances (weather, government directive, etc.), registration credits will be offered for the next edition.
